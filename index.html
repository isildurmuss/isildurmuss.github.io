About

Hey,

I'm Isil — a Geomatics Engineer and GeoAI developer based in Istanbul. This is where I share the systems I build at the intersection of spatial data, automation and large language models.

Story

I'm a Geomatics Engineering graduate from Istanbul Technical University, currently pursuing an MSc in the same field. Across my studies and internships I've focused on building fully autonomous, intelligent system architectures on the Python, FastAPI and Docker ecosystem.

Among the systems I've built are an autonomous GIS agent platform orchestrating 7 different LLMs simultaneously, a natural-language-powered spatial query engine (GeoChat/RAG), and an AI-powered automation pipeline that handles document validation, vector search and reviewer assignment end-to-end.

My engineering internship at the General Directorate of Mapping (Ministry of National Defense) instilled a discipline for data accuracy that I now carry into autonomous, intelligent GIS projects that push the boundaries of what AI can do spatially.

End-to-end web platform parsing natural-language spatial queries into live map visualizations, bypassing traditional desktop GIS software.
